Hermes personal automation

This site hosts the public homepage and policy pages for a private, single-user automation client that connects to its owner's own Google account.

  • The app is operated by one person, for his own mailbox, calendar and drive. It is not offered to the public.
  • It is not an app store product, it has no other users, and no third party has access to its credentials.
  • Access is limited to: reading and drafting email, reading calendar events, and reading or writing documents and spreadsheets that belong to the owner.

Read the privacy policy or the terms of use.
