Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

What this is

This policy covers a private automation client ("the app") that runs on the personal computer of its owner and connects to that owner's own Google account. The app has exactly one user: the account owner.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's explicit consent, the app may read and draft messages in the owner's Gmail account, read events in the owner's Google Calendar, and read or write the owner's own Google Drive files, Docs and Sheets. It accesses nothing else, and it never accesses another person's account.

How data is used

Data is used solely to carry out tasks the owner requests on his own machine, such as summarising his own incoming mail or drafting a reply for him to review. No automated decision-making is applied to anyone else.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer in a file readable only by his user account. Message content is processed on that machine and is not sold, published, or shared with any third party. Nothing is transferred to advertisers, data brokers or analytics services.

Third-party services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately ends all API access. Local credentials can be deleted from his computer at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy: the owner of this account, reachable at the support address listed on the application's consent screen, or via github.com/BenjaminTia.
